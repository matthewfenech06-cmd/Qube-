One Floor, Thirty Years

For three decades QUBE was a beloved one-floor bar in the heart of Paceville, the kind of place everyone on the island had a night in at least once.

It has now been rebuilt from the ground up: four floors, one name, and an experience unlike anything Malta has seen. Rebranded, revived and refined.
